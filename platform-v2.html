04 — Security

Security and Compliance, built in.

Ascending had been founded on the conviction that European companies and governments are becoming dependent on US AI infrastructure at exactly the moment AI becomes the core of how organisations work. Hence security and compliance to the highest European standards is non-negotiable in every piece of our technology.

Compliance
