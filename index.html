01 — Challenges

AI productivity platforms are not sufficient

A setup on Claude, ChatGPT, Copilot or Langdock without a complementary knowledge base is not enough for companies where knowledge is proprietary and dynamic, and insights must be accurate and auditable. We hear the same stories over and over again.

01

Core systems are not connected

Copilot, Claude and ChatGPT reach Drive and SharePoint. They don’t reach AFAS, DATEV, or the vertical systems many European companies actually run on.

02

Knowledge decay is now exponential

Documents multiply and versions diverge. What was previously a filing problem is now an input: agents draw conclusions, take actions and generate “new knowledge” from contradictory sources, then feed that output back in as a source of its own.

03

Insights are not reliable

The system reasons over what it happened to retrieve — not an exhaustive scan, and not checked against what is outdated or contradicted elsewhere. It returns an answer and a few citations, never the full list of relevant source documents. The answer can sound complete, but rarely is.

04

Insufficient understanding

The system matches words, not the relationships between them, so two connected facts remain two separate answers. When someone connects them anyway, that insight stays personal. Organisational knowledge does not accumulate.

05

Silent exposure of sensitive data

Access control is enforced at the source system, and nowhere else. This exposes sensitive information the moment it is pulled into a summary, a prompt, or an agent’s memory — extracted, recombined, and no longer covered by its source permissions.

06

Admins cannot enforce governance

Usage is not measured, model choice is not supervised, and quality is judged anecdotally. Spend is measured reactively or not at all and nobody can assess its actual impact.

07

Knowledge inside a black box

Knowledge sits in the system as embeddings, legible to a machine but not to a person. Every question goes back through the black box. There is no way to verify a claim, review what is stored, or simply browse.

08

Loss of control

The knowledge that used to be a company’s hardest-to-copy advantage now accumulates inside a vendor’s model rather than inside the company. Companies risk losing their edge when the subscription ends.
